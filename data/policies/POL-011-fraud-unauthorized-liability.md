# POL-011 — Fraud: Consumer Liability Tiers

*Northwind Bank (fictional institution — synthetic policy for demo purposes).*
Applies to: unauthorised electronic fund transfers on **debit** accounts.

## Clause 11.1 — Tier A — reported within 2 business days

Where the customer reports the loss, theft, or unauthorised use within **2 business days** of
discovery, maximum customer liability is **USD 50**.

## Clause 11.2 — Tier B — reported after 2 business days

Where the report is made after 2 business days but within **60 calendar days** of the statement
transmittal date, maximum customer liability rises to **USD 500** for transfers that occurred after
the 2-day mark and could have been prevented by timely notice.

## Clause 11.3 — Tier C — reported after 60 days

Transfers appearing on a statement more than **60 calendar days** before notice carry **unlimited**
customer liability. This tier is applied only where the bank can show the loss would have been
avoided by timely notice.

## Clause 11.4 — Zero-liability override

Northwind's commercial zero-liability policy waives Tier A and Tier B liability entirely for
verified fraud where the account is in good standing and the customer has filed no more than two
prior claims in the preceding 12 months. Tier C is **not** waived.

> **Distinguish from POL-010.** POL-010 sets whether a claim is *timely*. This policy sets what the
> customer *pays* once timeliness is established. A claim can be timely and still carry liability.
