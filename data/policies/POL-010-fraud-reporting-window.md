# POL-010 — Fraud: Reporting Windows

*Northwind Bank (fictional institution — synthetic policy for demo purposes).*

## Clause 10.1 — Standard reporting window

An unauthorised transaction must be reported within **60 calendar days** of the transmittal date of
the periodic statement on which it first appeared. Claims reported after this window may be denied
on timeliness grounds alone.

## Clause 10.2 — Extenuating circumstances

The 60-day window is extended by a reasonable period where the delay was caused by extended travel,
hospitalisation, or a natural disaster affecting the customer's address of record. Extensions are
granted case by case and must be documented in the claim record.

## Clause 10.3 — Lost or stolen access device

Where an access device (card, credentials, or token) is lost or stolen, the customer should report
within **2 business days** of discovery. Reporting after that point does not bar the claim but
directly affects the customer's liability ceiling — see POL-011.

## Clause 10.4 — Continuing series

Where unauthorised transactions form a continuing series by the same actor, the reporting window
runs from the statement showing the **first** transaction in the series, not the most recent.

> **Distinguish from POL-002 Clause 2.3.** That 60-day window governs credit card *billing error*
> notice and requires writing. This window governs *fraud* reporting and accepts oral notice.
