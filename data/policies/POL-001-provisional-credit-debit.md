# POL-001 — Provisional Credit: Debit Card Disputes

*Northwind Bank (fictional institution — synthetic policy for demo purposes).*
Governs: consumer **debit card** and ATM transactions under Regulation E.

## Clause 1.1 — Provisional credit deadline

Where a consumer reports an unauthorized electronic fund transfer on a **debit** card and the
investigation cannot be completed within **10 business days** of receiving notice, the bank
**must** issue provisional credit for the disputed amount, including any associated fees, while
the investigation continues.

## Clause 1.2 — Investigation window

The standard investigation window is **45 calendar days** from the date of notice. This extends to
**90 calendar days** where the transaction was initiated outside the United States, was a
point-of-sale debit transaction, or occurred within 30 days of the first deposit to the account.

## Clause 1.3 — Notice of results

The consumer must be notified of the investigation outcome within **3 business days** of its
completion. Where the claim is denied, the bank must provide a written explanation and inform the
consumer of the right to request the documents relied upon.

## Clause 1.4 — Reversal of provisional credit

Provisional credit may be reversed only after the consumer has been given **5 business days'**
advance written notice of the reversal and its effective date.

> **Distinguish from POL-002.** These provisional-credit obligations apply to debit only. Credit
> card disputes follow Regulation Z and carry **no** provisional-credit requirement.
