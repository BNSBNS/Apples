# POL-023 — Merchant Dispute: Cancelled Recurring Billing

*Northwind Bank (fictional institution — synthetic policy for demo purposes).*
Category: merchant error.

## Clause 23.1 — Preconditions

The customer must have cancelled the recurring arrangement with the merchant **before** the
disputed billing date, and must be able to state the cancellation date and method. Where the
merchant issued a cancellation confirmation, it must be supplied.

## Clause 23.2 — Evidence where no confirmation exists

Absent a confirmation, the claim may still proceed on the customer's written attestation of the
cancellation date and method, provided the account shows **no usage** of the service after the
stated cancellation date.

## Clause 23.3 — Scope of credit

Credit is limited to billing cycles **after** the cancellation date. Cycles billed before
cancellation are valid charges and must not be credited, even where the customer disputes the
entire series.

## Clause 23.4 — Prospective blocking

On resolving the claim, a stop instruction must be placed against future authorisations from that
merchant for the card. This does not terminate the customer's contractual obligation to the
merchant.

> **Distinguish from POL-022.** Identical amounts recurring on a monthly cycle are the *expected*
> pattern here, not evidence of duplication.
