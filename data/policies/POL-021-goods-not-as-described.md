# POL-021 — Merchant Dispute: Goods Not as Described or Defective

*Northwind Bank (fictional institution — synthetic policy for demo purposes).*
Category: merchant error. **Not** an unauthorised-transaction claim.

## Clause 21.1 — Preconditions

The customer must confirm the transaction was authorised, that the goods or services were received,
and that they materially differ from their description or are defective. The customer must have
attempted return or remedy with the merchant and been refused or received no response within
**15 calendar days**.

## Clause 21.2 — No waiting period

Unlike POL-020, **no waiting period applies** once the merchant has refused remedy or the 15-day
response period has lapsed. The claim may be filed immediately.

## Clause 21.3 — Filing deadline

Claims must be filed within **120 calendar days** of receipt of the goods, and in no case more than
**540 calendar days** after the transaction date.

## Clause 21.4 — Evidence required

The item description as advertised at time of purchase, photographs or a written account of the
discrepancy, and the merchant's refusal or evidence of non-response. Return tracking is required
only where the merchant accepted a return.

> **Distinguish from POL-020.** POL-020 governs goods that never arrived and imposes a 15-day
> waiting period from the delivery date. This policy governs goods that arrived but were wrong, and
> imposes no waiting period once remedy has been refused.
