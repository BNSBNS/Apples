# POL-003 — Provisional Credit: Exceptions and Withholding

*Northwind Bank (fictional institution — synthetic policy for demo purposes).*
Modifies: POL-001 (debit provisional credit).

## Clause 3.1 — New account exception

Where the disputed transaction occurred within **30 calendar days** of the first deposit to the
account, the bank may withhold provisional credit and extend the investigation window to **20
business days** before credit becomes due.

## Clause 3.2 — Written confirmation not received

Where the bank requested written confirmation of an oral notice and did not receive it within
**10 business days** of the request, provisional credit may be withheld. The investigation must
nonetheless proceed to completion.

## Clause 3.3 — Prior claim pattern

Where the customer has filed **three or more** unauthorised-transaction claims in the preceding
**12 months**, provisional credit is withheld pending manual review by Fraud Operations. This is a
review trigger, not a denial — see POL-031 for escalation handling.

## Clause 3.4 — Amount threshold

Provisional credit for a single claim exceeding **USD 5,000** requires supervisory approval before
issuance regardless of any other provision in POL-001.

> **Note.** Exceptions in this policy narrow POL-001 only. They have no effect on the Regulation Z
> credit card timelines in POL-002.
