# POL-002 — Billing Error Resolution: Credit Card Disputes

*Northwind Bank (fictional institution — synthetic policy for demo purposes).*
Governs: consumer **credit card** accounts under Regulation Z.

## Clause 2.1 — No provisional credit obligation

Credit card billing error disputes carry **no provisional credit requirement**. The disputed amount
is instead placed in a **non-accruing suspended state**: the consumer is not required to pay the
disputed amount, and no interest or late fee may be assessed against it, while the dispute is open.

## Clause 2.2 — Acknowledgement and resolution windows

Written notice of a billing error must be acknowledged within **30 calendar days** of receipt. The
dispute must be resolved within **two complete billing cycles**, and in no case later than **90
calendar days** from receipt of the notice.

## Clause 2.3 — Consumer notice requirements

To trigger these protections the consumer's notice must arrive in writing within **60 calendar
days** of the statement on which the disputed item first appeared. Telephone notice does not
trigger the obligations in this policy.

## Clause 2.4 — Restoration on a resolved claim

Where the dispute resolves in the consumer's favour, the amount, together with any related finance
charges and fees, must be credited within **1 billing cycle**.

> **Distinguish from POL-001.** The 10-business-day provisional-credit rule is a **debit**
> (Regulation E) obligation and does not apply to credit card accounts.
