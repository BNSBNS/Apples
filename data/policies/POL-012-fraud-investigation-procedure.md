# POL-012 — Fraud: Investigation Procedure

*Northwind Bank (fictional institution — synthetic policy for demo purposes).*

## Clause 12.1 — Evidence to collect

Every unauthorised-transaction investigation must gather: the transaction authorisation record;
device and channel metadata (IP, device fingerprint, terminal ID); the cardholder's stated location
at the time; the merchant category code; and the customer's transaction history for the preceding
**90 days**.

## Clause 12.2 — Indicators supporting the claim

The following support a finding of genuine fraud: geographic impossibility between transactions;
merchant category inconsistent with the customer's established pattern; a card-present transaction
while the card is confirmed in the customer's possession; and rapid-succession authorisations at
escalating amounts.

## Clause 12.3 — Indicators contradicting the claim

The following weigh against the claim: delivery to the customer's address of record; use of a
device previously enrolled by the customer; a transaction preceded by successful step-up
authentication; and any prior undisputed transaction with the same merchant.

## Clause 12.4 — Findings and documentation

The investigator must record a written finding citing the specific indicators relied upon. A denial
citing no indicator from Clause 12.3 is not a supportable denial and must be returned for rework.

> **Distinguish from POL-010 and POL-011.** This policy governs *how* an investigation is conducted.
> Timeliness is POL-010; liability amount is POL-011.
