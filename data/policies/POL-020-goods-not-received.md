# POL-020 — Merchant Dispute: Goods or Services Not Received

*Northwind Bank (fictional institution — synthetic policy for demo purposes).*
Category: merchant error. **Not** an unauthorised-transaction claim.

## Clause 20.1 — Preconditions

The customer must confirm that the transaction was authorised by them, that the expected delivery
or performance date has passed, and that they have attempted to resolve the matter with the
merchant directly. Merchant contact must be attempted at least **once** and documented.

## Clause 20.2 — Waiting period

A claim may not be filed until **15 calendar days** after the expected delivery date. Where the
merchant has issued a revised delivery date in writing, the waiting period runs from the revised
date.

## Clause 20.3 — Filing deadline

Claims must be filed within **120 calendar days** of the expected delivery date, and in no case
more than **540 calendar days** after the transaction date.

## Clause 20.4 — Evidence required

Order confirmation, the merchant's stated delivery date, proof of the customer's attempt to contact
the merchant, and any carrier tracking showing non-delivery.

> **Distinguish from POL-021.** This policy covers goods that **never arrived**. Goods that arrived
> but were wrong, damaged, or misrepresented fall under POL-021 and carry a different waiting
> period and evidence set.
